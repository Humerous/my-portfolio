About

Technical craft,
creative instinct.

I’m a developer with an established background in technology, film and media—and the perspective of a business owner.

Since founding Video Image Technologies in 2016, I’ve learned to move from an idea to something real: understand the audience, shape the story, solve the technical problem and deliver with care. Today I bring that same end-to-end thinking to web products.

I enjoy the space between disciplines: where design decisions meet application logic, and where a functional build still has to feel clear, human and memorable.
